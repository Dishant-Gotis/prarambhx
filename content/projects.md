# Featured Projects

## Project Alpha: Precision Agriculture UAV
**Category:** Autonomous Systems  
**Client:** AgriTech Research Institute  
**Challenge:** Design a multi-spectral imaging drone for crop health monitoring across 500+ acre farms with autonomous waypoint navigation.

**Solution:**
- Custom hexacopter platform with 45-minute flight endurance
- Integrated NDVI camera with real-time data streaming
- Autonomous mission planning with obstacle detection
- Ground control software for analytics and mapping

**Outcome:** Deployed across 12 farms, reducing manual surveying time by 80% and improving yield prediction accuracy.

---

## Project Nexus: Open-Source Flight Controller
**Category:** Flight Controller R&D  
**Challenge:** Develop a modular, educator-friendly autopilot system for university research labs and student projects.

**Solution:**
- STM32-based flight controller with open firmware architecture
- Custom PID tuning interface and telemetry dashboard
- Support for quadcopter, hexacopter, and fixed-wing configurations
- Detailed documentation and example projects

**Outcome:** Adopted by 15+ universities and used in 200+ student research projects.

---

## Project Sentinel: Autonomous Surveillance Drone
**Category:** Defense & Security  
**Client:** Border Security Research Division  
**Challenge:** Create a long-endurance fixed-wing UAV with autonomous patrol capability and real-time encrypted video transmission.

**Solution:**
- Fixed-wing platform with 3-hour endurance
- Custom autopilot with GPS-denied navigation backup (optical flow + IMU)
- Encrypted telemetry and HD video streaming
- Autonomous takeoff, patrol, and landing

**Outcome:** Successfully completed 50+ field trials with 98% mission completion rate.

---

## Project Swarm: Multi-Agent Coordination
**Category:** Research Prototype  
**Partner:** Robotics & AI Lab, IIT Bombay  
**Challenge:** Develop decentralized swarm algorithms for coordinated flight of 10+ drones without central control.

**Solution:**
- Custom communication protocol for inter-drone messaging
- Distributed consensus algorithms for formation control
- Collision avoidance using proximity sensors
- Simulation-to-reality pipeline for algorithm validation

**Outcome:** Published research paper and live demonstration with 12-drone swarm performing coordinated maneuvers.

---

## Project Hydra: VTOL Hybrid Platform
**Category:** Custom Engineering  
**Client:** Geospatial Surveying Company  
**Challenge:** Build a hybrid drone with vertical takeoff capability and fixed-wing endurance for large-area mapping.

**Solution:**
- Tilt-rotor VTOL design with seamless transition control
- Custom flight controller with mode-switching autopilot
- LiDAR integration for 3D terrain mapping
- 90-minute flight time with 2kg payload capacity

**Outcome:** Reduced surveying costs by 60% compared to traditional helicopter-based methods.
