# Training Pathways

## Pathway 1: Drone Pilot Track
**For:** Students, hobbyists, and aspiring commercial pilots

**Level 1 – Beginner Pilot**
- Drone basics and flight theory
- Manual flight training
- Safety and regulations

**Level 2 – Advanced Pilot**
- Autonomous mission planning
- Payload operations
- Emergency procedures

**Level 3 – Commercial Pilot**
- DGCA RPL certification prep
- Professional operations
- Client delivery and reporting

**Duration:** 12 Weeks (Full Track)  
**Certification:** DGCA Remote Pilot License + PrarambhX Commercial Pilot Certificate

---

## Pathway 2: Drone Builder & Engineer Track
**For:** Engineering students and technical professionals

**Level 1 – Hardware Fundamentals**
- Component selection and assembly
- Electronics and power systems
- First build and test flight

**Level 2 – Flight Controller Programming**
- Embedded firmware development
- Sensor integration and calibration
- PID tuning and control theory

**Level 3 – Advanced Systems**
- Custom autopilot design
- Telemetry and ground control software
- System testing and validation

**Duration:** 20 Weeks (Full Track)  
**Certification:** Flight Controller Developer Certificate + Drone Systems Engineer Certificate

---

## Pathway 3: Autonomous Systems & AI Track
**For:** Software engineers, AI/ML practitioners, researchers

**Level 1 – Robotics Fundamentals**
- ROS (Robot Operating System)
- Coordinate systems and transformations
- Sensor data processing

**Level 2 – Computer Vision for Drones**
- Object detection and tracking
- Visual odometry and SLAM
- Edge deployment (Jetson, RPi)

**Level 3 – AI-Driven Autonomy**
- Reinforcement learning for control
- Path planning algorithms
- Swarm coordination

**Duration:** 18 Weeks (Full Track)  
**Certification:** Autonomous Systems Engineer Certificate

# Training Format

## Blended Learning
- **Online Theory:** Video lectures, reading materials, quizzes (self-paced or live sessions)
- **In-Person Labs:** Hands-on hardware sessions at PrarambhX facilities
- **Remote Labs:** Access to cloud-based simulation environments and virtual workspaces

## Cohort-Based Programs
Join a batch of 15–20 students with fixed start dates, scheduled live sessions, and peer collaboration.

**Benefits:**
- Structured learning timeline
- Group projects and hackathons
- Dedicated instructor support
- Networking opportunities

## Self-Paced Learning
Access course materials anytime, complete assignments at your own speed, and book lab sessions flexibly.

**Benefits:**
- Learn around your schedule
- Repeat modules as needed
- Lifetime access to materials
