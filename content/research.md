# Research & Innovation

## Headline
Advancing the Science of Autonomous Flight

## Subtext
Our research pushes the boundaries of drone technology through deep exploration of autonomy, control systems, swarm intelligence, and AI-driven navigation. We collaborate with academic institutions and publish our findings to advance the field.

---

# Research Focus Areas

## Autonomous Navigation
GPS-denied flight using visual odometry, SLAM, and sensor fusion. Exploring robust localization in indoor, urban canyon, and GPS-jammed environments.

## Swarm Intelligence
Decentralized multi-agent coordination, formation control, and emergent behavior for large-scale drone swarms without centralized orchestration.

## AI & Machine Learning
Onboard computer vision for object detection, path planning using reinforcement learning, and adaptive control systems that learn from flight data.

## Flight Dynamics & Control
Advanced control algorithms including model predictive control (MPC), adaptive PID, and nonlinear control for aggressive maneuvers and unstable platforms.

## Energy Optimization
Battery management systems, power-efficient flight planning, and hybrid propulsion research to extend endurance and payload capacity.

## Human-Drone Interaction
Gesture-based control, voice commands, and AR/VR interfaces for intuitive drone operation in collaborative scenarios.

# Active Research Projects

## Autonomous Indoor Navigation Using Visual-Inertial Odometry
**Status:** Ongoing  
**Partners:** IIT Delhi Robotics Lab  
**Timeline:** 2024–2026

**Objective:** Enable precise drone navigation in GPS-denied indoor environments using camera and IMU fusion.

**Approach:**
- Custom VIO algorithm combining feature tracking and inertial measurements
- Real-time implementation on embedded hardware (Jetson Nano)
- Integration with existing flight controller for closed-loop position control

**Current Progress:** Algorithm validated in simulation; hardware testing underway.

---

## Decentralized Swarm Coordination with Emergent Behavior
**Status:** Published  
**Partners:** Robotics Institute, NIT Trichy  
**Publication:** IEEE ICRA 2025

**Objective:** Develop algorithms for self-organizing drone swarms that adapt to dynamic environments without central control.

**Approach:**
- Bio-inspired algorithms based on flocking and consensus theory
- Inter-drone communication using custom mesh network protocol
- Simulation with 50+ agents, validated with 12-drone hardware swarm

**Outcome:** Successfully demonstrated formation flight, obstacle avoidance, and target tracking with 12 drones.

---

## Reinforcement Learning for Aggressive Drone Maneuvers
**Status:** Ongoing  
**Collaborators:** Machine Learning Research Group, IIIT Hyderabad  
**Timeline:** 2025–2027

**Objective:** Train neural network policies for high-speed racing maneuvers, flip recovery, and turbulence adaptation.

**Approach:**
- Simulated environments using PyBullet and custom drone dynamics models
- PPO and SAC algorithms for policy optimization
- Sim-to-real transfer using domain randomization

**Current Progress:** Policies achieving 85% success rate in simulation; initiating real-world testing.

---

## Hybrid Propulsion System for Extended Endurance
**Status:** Prototype Phase  
**Funding:** DST SERB Grant  
**Timeline:** 2023–2025

**Objective:** Design a hybrid gas-electric propulsion system to extend multi-rotor endurance from 30 minutes to 2+ hours.

**Approach:**
- Small IC engine generator paired with lithium polymer battery
- Custom power management electronics for seamless switching
- Lightweight carbon fiber airframe optimized for hybrid system

**Current Progress:** Prototype achieving 95-minute flight with 1.5kg payload.

---

## Onboard Object Detection for Delivery Drones
**Status:** Field Testing  
**Partners:** E-commerce Logistics Partner  
**Timeline:** 2024–2025

**Objective:** Enable autonomous precision landing on dynamic targets (rooftops, balconies) using computer vision.

**Approach:**
- YOLOv8 model trained on landing pad dataset
- Edge deployment on NVIDIA Jetson for real-time inference
- Visual servoing for closed-loop landing control

**Current Progress:** 92% detection accuracy in field tests across 200+ landings.

---

## Fault-Tolerant Control with Motor Failure Resilience
**Status:** Published  
**Publication:** Journal of Unmanned Aerial Systems (2024)  

**Objective:** Develop control strategies for hexacopters to maintain stable flight after single motor failure.

**Approach:**
- Control allocation algorithms redistributing thrust to remaining motors
- Real-time fault detection using motor current and vibration analysis
- Emergency landing procedures with degraded controllability

**Outcome:** Demonstrated successful controlled descent in 95% of simulated motor failure scenarios.
