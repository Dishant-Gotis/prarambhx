# About PrarambhX

## Vision
To democratize advanced drone technology through innovation, education, and open collaboration—empowering researchers, students, and industries to unlock the full potential of autonomous aerial systems.

# Mission

We exist to:
- Design and build high-performance drone systems that solve real-world challenges
- Advance the state of the art in flight control, autonomy, and aerial robotics through rigorous R&D
- Train the next generation of drone engineers, pilots, and innovators with world-class education programs
- Foster a collaborative ecosystem where academia, industry, and startups co-create the future of unmanned flight

---

# Our Story

PrarambhX began in 2019 as a research initiative by aerospace engineers and embedded systems designers who saw a gap: exceptional drone hardware existed, but few organizations combined deep technical expertise with accessible education and custom system design.

We started in a university lab, building flight controllers for research projects. Students asked to learn. Industry partners asked for custom solutions. Research groups asked for collaboration.

Today, PrarambhX is a full-spectrum drone technology company—designing proprietary autopilot systems, conducting cutting-edge R&D, and running India's most comprehensive drone training programs.

We've trained over 1,200 students, deployed systems in agriculture, surveying, defense, and disaster response, and contributed to research projects exploring swarm intelligence, computer vision, and autonomous navigation.

**Our name—PrarambhX—means "The Beginning of X":** the unknown breakthrough, the next innovation, the start of something transformative. That spirit drives everything we do.
